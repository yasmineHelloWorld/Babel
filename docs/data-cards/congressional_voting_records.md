# Congressional Voting Records dataset

- **Source:** UCI Machine Learning Repository, Congressional Voting Records (ID 105), 1984 U.S. House votes.
- **Rows:** 435 House member voting records.
- **Columns:** 16 categorical issue votes, each encoded as yea (`y`), nay (`n`), or unknown (`?`).
- **Label:** party affiliation (`democrat` or `republican`).
- **Usage:** real-world categorical benchmark for rate-accuracy curves and optional held-out vote-combination generalization. Preserve unknown votes as an explicit category.
- **Limitations:** small and historically specific; party and voting patterns reflect the 1984 U.S. House and should not be generalized to current political alignments.
